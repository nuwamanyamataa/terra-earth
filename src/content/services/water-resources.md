faqs:
  - q: How deep is a typical borehole?
    a: It depends on the local geology and the water table. A hydrogeological survey before drilling tells us the likely depth and yield, so you are not paying to drill in the wrong place.
  - q: Can a solar pump lift water up a hill?
    a: Yes. We size the pump, panels and pipework for the total height and distance, so water reaches the highest point you need — for example, a storage tank on a hilltop that feeds irrigation by gravity.
  - q: Why lithium batteries instead of gel batteries?
    a: Lithium batteries use more of their stored energy (up to about 90% depth of discharge versus about 50% for gel), last for 3,000–5,000 charge cycles instead of 500–1,000, and are lighter and more compact.

Terra Earth Engineering has extensive expertise in water resources planning, development and sustainable management. We support work that improves access to water, makes irrigation more productive and protects catchments for the long term.

Solar-powered water systems are a growing part of our work: from floating solar pumps on open water to systems that pump water to the top of a hill for gravity-fed irrigation. By prioritising sustainable abstraction, efficient water use and long-term reliability, we help improve water security, agricultural productivity and community resilience.
