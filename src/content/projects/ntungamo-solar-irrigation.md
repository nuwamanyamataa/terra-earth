## The challenge

The client wanted to irrigate 41 acres of a hillside farm near Itojo in Ntungamo District. The water source sits well below the garden, so water had to be pumped to the highest point of the hill before it could serve the whole area. The farm had no grid power at the source.

## Our solution

Terra Earth designed, supplied and installed a solar-powered pumping system sized for the full height and distance to the hilltop, together with a drag-hose irrigation system covering the 41 acres. Running on solar power keeps operating costs low and removes the need for fuel deliveries.

The contract set a clear performance requirement: the system must deliver water to the highest point of the hill, as agreed with the client.

## The result

A solar-powered irrigation system designed to water the whole 41 acres from the hilltop, with no fuel costs, backed by an 18-month warranty on equipment and workmanship.
