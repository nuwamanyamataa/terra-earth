## The challenge

The school needed reliable power for 20 classroom lights, outdoor security lights, sockets and a 50-inch smart TV, day and night — about 7 kWh of energy a day with a peak load of 541 W.

## Our solution

A stand-alone solar system sized from a full load analysis: three 615 W panels, a 1.8 kW hybrid inverter and two 3.58 kWh lithium batteries, plus six solar flood lights around the classrooms. Staff were trained on operating the system.
